# Self-Model Onboarding Interview

Fill this in at your own pace. It seeds two things: a first picture of who
you are now and a first sketch of who you want to become — the two models
the whole second brain works to close the gap between.

## How to use this

- **No right answers.** Half-formed, contradictory, "I don't know but maybe…" is exactly the good stuff.
- **Reach for the true answer, not the impressive one.** The system is only as honest as this file.
- **Skip anything that doesn't land** — leave it blank or write "pass." Ramble where you want to.
- **Contradict yourself freely.** If two answers clash, that's a held tension, not a mistake — it's some of the richest signal.
- Write under each prompt (replace the `…`). Length doesn't matter.
- Pause and come back whenever. Send it back partial if you like.

## What happens when you send this back (or drop it in the vault)

Once ingested (Phase 2), your answers become:

- `self/desired/` — your north-star, an "I am someone who…" identity sketch, aspirations, and no-gos.
- `self/current/` — first-pass traits (working-style, energy-mood, decision-style, values, anti-patterns), each stored at **low confidence** so the system treats them as hypotheses to confirm with real data, not facts.
- `self/profile-seed.md` — this whole thing, preserved and editable forever.
- A short list of the **tensions** noticed between where you are and where you want to be — the first gaps the engine will work on.

Send it back however complete it is. Partial is fine — you can always deepen
it later.
